## Marriage — The Honest Picture

Let me be direct. This marriage is not easy. It was never going to be.

*7th lord Guru (Jupiter) in 6th house, retrograde.* The lord of marriage sitting in the house of enemies, conflicts, and daily struggle. This is the single most defining factor. It means:

•⁠  ⁠The marriage requires continuous work. There is no "cruise control" phase where everything just flows.
•⁠  ⁠Arguments happen about everyday things — money, chores, children, in-laws, schedules. Not dramatic betrayals. Just grinding, repetitive friction.
•⁠  ⁠Both partners feel they're giving more than they're getting. Both are probably right — because the 6th house takes more than it gives.

*Ketu in 7th house (Mula Nakshatra).* Ketu detaches from whatever it touches. In the marriage house:

•⁠  ⁠There are phases — could be weeks, could be months — where they feel like roommates, not partners. Going through the motions. Polite but distant. No fighting, no loving. Just... existing together.
•⁠  ⁠She may wonder if she married the right person during these phases. This is Ketu's illusion. The detachment passes and connection returns. But while it lasts, it's lonely.
•⁠  ⁠Mula nakshatra = "the root." Ketu here occasionally uproots the foundations of the marriage. A crisis, a revelation, a fight that goes too far. Then both partners are forced to rebuild from the root. Painful but ultimately strengthening.

*What the husband is like in the marriage:*

•⁠  ⁠He works hard. Very hard. And he expects appreciation for it. He shows love through providing, not through words or romance. He comes home tired and wants peace, not deep conversation. She wants connection. He wants quiet. This mismatch is daily.
•⁠  ⁠He is stubborn. Dhanusu 7th house = he thinks his way is right. Not in a cruel way — in a principled way. "This is how things should be done." Arguing with his principles is exhausting because he has complete conviction.
•⁠  ⁠He doesn't open up. Jupiter in Viruchigam (Scorpio) = deep waters, strong walls. She may have tried many times to get him to share his feelings. He deflects, changes the subject, or says "everything is fine" when it obviously isn't.
•⁠  ⁠He is loyal. That's not in question. Anuradha nakshatra = he won't betray the marriage. But loyalty and emotional intimacy are not the same thing.

*What SHE brings to the marriage that's difficult:*

•⁠  ⁠Restlessness. He feels it even if she doesn't say it. He senses that she's not fully content, and it makes him feel inadequate.
•⁠  ⁠Sharpness under stress. When she snaps, her words land hard. He may have learned to shut down rather than engage when she's in that mode.
•⁠  ⁠Wanting more. More talk, more romance, more adventure, more meaning. He doesn't know how to give "more" of something he can barely define.
•⁠  ⁠Comparing. Rahu on Lagnam may make her compare her marriage to others. Other couples seem happier, more connected, more exciting. The comparison is usually unfair — she's seeing their highlights, not their private struggles.

*What holds this marriage together:*

•⁠  ⁠Saturn exalted aspecting 7th = structural integrity. This marriage is built on strong foundations — duty, commitment, shared responsibility. It doesn't break easily.
•⁠  ⁠Venus in 2nd = genuine family love. When they're not fighting, the home is warm. Good food, comfortable environment, family rituals.
•⁠  ⁠Ketu's karmic bond = at a level beneath conscious awareness, they recognize each other. There are moments — rare, fleeting — of complete understanding where words are unnecessary. These moments keep the marriage alive.
•⁠  ⁠Children. If they have children, the kids are the strongest glue. Both partners love the children deeply and that shared love is the common ground even when everything else feels distant.

*Current state (2020-2027, Mars Mahadasha):*

This is the marriage's hardest period. Mars as 6th lord running the Mahadasha amplifies every conflict, every unspoken frustration, every health issue. Physical separation or emotional distance is likely higher than previous years. She may have seriously questioned the marriage during this period.

The good news: it ends March 2027. And within 2026, the August-December window (Mars-Moon) brings genuine emotional healing.
